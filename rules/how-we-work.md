# How we work

- Go step by step. Tell the plan first; the owner confirms what to do and not to do.
- Never create a document or file without the owner knowing: explain what it is, why, and how it helps later. Wait for a yes.
- Keep every document simple.
- No hallucinations, no mistakes. There is time to do it well.
- Before asking a question, re-read everything. Ask only what is not already answered or truly unclear. No repeated questions.
- The owner's personal name is never written in outputs.
- Names and lists first. Descriptions only after the names are confirmed.
- A missing item is written as "Needs: <item>" under the task name, with a short note on why it is needed and whether the task can still be done well without it. No questions about buying.
- Items used in platform tasks count as in stock, even if not in the inventory list.

## Robot and rigs
- Two-jaw grippers, 95 mm opening, six-zone bench. 4 recording rigs.
- Fans are excluded (too big).
- Drills are allowed, but keep their use simple.

## What kind of tasks
- Creative, manipulation-rich real tasks clients worldwide would buy. Not simple pick-and-place.
- Simple and doable, but never senseless.
- Never redo a task already on the platform. A platform task may appear only as a reverse.
- Always check whether a reverse is possible and add it.
- Real tasks (bottles, clothes, kitting, packing) come before electrical tasks.

## Camera
- Anything put into a box, bag or jar needs extra light so it shows on camera.
- Liquids are red or blue only. Prefer clear or white bags, not black.
- Nuts and bolts: the big black ones only.
